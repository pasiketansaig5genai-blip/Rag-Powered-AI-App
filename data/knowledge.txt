The reason why I chose to study MEng in Industrial Informatics at Hochschule Emden-Leer is that my 
academic and professional interests have shifted towards the traditional mechanical systems to integrated and 
intelligent technologies. My Mechanical Engineering degree, with a minor in Computer Science, gave me a 
keen interest in integrating physical systems with data-driven solutions. This orientation was supported by my 
capstone project on real-time predictive maintenance with machine learning, during which I dealt with sensor 
data and developed analytical models. Furthermore, my current role as a Junior AI Engineer at Valentius 
Kryptix has allowed me to build and deploy enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines and 
autonomous AI agents, while working with LLM APIs, semantic search, vector-index structures, and context 
engineering. My internship at Hindustan Shipyard and my experience at RDC Concrete also introduced me to 
automation, PLC systems, and Industry 4.0. Although these experiences reinforced my practical knowledge, 
they also revealed that I needed more theoretical knowledge in the area of system integration and intelligent 
control, which led me to continue my studies in this area. 
My studies in Mechanical Engineering at MVGR College of Engineering and a minor in Computer Science 
have enabled me to acquire a solid interdisciplinary base. I developed analytical and computational skills 
through coursework in thermodynamics, machine design, CAD/CAM, data structures, and database systems. 
I used this knowledge in various projects, such as an IoT-based smart traffic management system with 
OpenCV, and an industry project at Hindustan Shipyard, where I optimised a rod gearing mechanism, 
increasing the efficiency by 20%. My capstone project on real-time predictive maintenance further reinforced 
my interest in intelligent systems, where I used sensor data and a LightGBM model to predict machine health. 
Also, my study of alcoholic fuel blends in VCR diesel engines helped me to understand performance analysis 
and sustainability better. All these experiences are indicative of my capacity to combine theory and practical, 
real-world problem-solving. 
All of my internships, certifications, and leadership experience have contributed to my interdisciplinary 
approach to engineering and technology. My experience at Hindustan Shipyard Limited helped me to enhance

In my current role as a Junior AI Engineer at Valentius Kryptix, I have further strengthened my understanding

Previous work in Exposys Data Labs and Smartbridge exposed me to web development and enterprise
